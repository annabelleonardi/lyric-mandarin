How it works

From playlist to fluency in three steps

No copying, no pasting, no switching tabs. The lesson happens inside the player you already use.

1

Play any song

Open a Chinese song on YouTube or NetEase Cloud Music. LyricMandarin pulls the official timed lyrics and follows the line that's playing — automatically.

2

Read along in color

Each line gets pinyin and a translation, with every word color-coded by difficulty: green for beginner HSK, amber for intermediate, purple for advanced.

3

Save & review

Click any word for an instant dictionary card, save it to your deck, and export everything as a CSV that imports straight into Anki.

Features

Everything a learner needs, nothing they don't

Built for the moment you actually want to look something up — while the song is playing.

Synced lyrics, automatically

Fetches official timed lyrics from NetEase — by song ID on NetEase, or by title match on YouTube — and highlights each line in sync with the music. Scrolling pages, iframes included.

HSK color grading

Every word is graded on the HSK 1–9 scale and colored accordingly, so you instantly see which words are within reach and which are a stretch.

AI cultural insights

One click explains the slang, idioms, and metaphors behind a line — like why the moon stands for longing in Chinese pop lyrics.

Instant word lookup

Click any colored word for pinyin, its HSK level, and a context-aware definition — the meaning it carries in that exact lyric, not just the textbook one.

Flashcard deck + Anki export

Words you save collect into a deck right in the popup. Export the whole deck as a CSV that imports straight into Anki for spaced-repetition review.

Privacy-first by design

Lyrics, saved words, and your API key are stored locally in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere except the AI provider you choose.

Install

Set up in under two minutes

LyricMandarin installs directly into Chrome — no store waitlist, no account.

Download LyricMandarin for Chrome
1

Get the package

Download the zip and unzip it — you'll get a folder named build/chrome-mv3-prod. That's all you need.

2

Open chrome://extensions

Paste that address into Chrome and switch on Developer mode in the top-right corner.

3

Load & pin

Click Load unpacked, select the folder, and pin LyricMandarin to your toolbar. That's it — you're in.

Then open any Chinese song and pick your target HSK level in Settings. No API key? LyricMandarin works out of the box with built-in sample content — add your DeepSeek or OpenAI key anytime to unlock unlimited AI lookups.

Your data never leaves your browser

Lyrics, saved words, and your API key live in local extension storage (chrome.storage.local). Nothing is sent anywhere except the AI provider you choose — and without a key, the extension doesn't need the network at all.
